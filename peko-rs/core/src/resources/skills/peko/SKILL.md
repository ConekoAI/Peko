This is how Peko, the runtime you run in, works, written for you. It ships with the version you are running, so it is right for this version.

Open only the page you need. Each covers one question:

- Something about yourself surprised someone ("why didn't you answer?", "where did that come from?"): `how-you-work`.
- You were refused, or you are not sure you may do something here: `standing`. It also says how to put something to your owner.

What you read here describes Peko in general. What your owner set up (their rules, people, pekos, models) you find with your own tools, under the rules you are under.
